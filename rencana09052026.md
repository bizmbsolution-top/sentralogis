## 🎯 Target Hari Ini (09 Mei 2026)

### Milestone: **Gross Margin Visibility per Job Order**

> **Definisi Gross Margin:**
> ```
> Gross Margin = Revenue (Deal Price) − COGS
> COGS = Purchase Price + Approved Extra Costs (surcharge + reimbursement yang ditanggung perusahaan)
> Gross Margin % = (Gross Margin / Revenue) × 100
> ```
