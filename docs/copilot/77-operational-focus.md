# 77. Operational Focus

By layering Priority Scoring and Focus Queues on top of Operational Insights, the Sentralogis Copilot achieves its ultimate objective: telling human operators exactly where to focus.

## Rule Enforcement
Crucially, despite analyzing urgency and filtering critical jobs, this layer contains **zero execution logic**. It remains a read-only decision support tool. Autonomous mutation of aggregates is strictly prohibited. The Copilot guides the operator to the burning fire, but the operator must pull the trigger to extinguish it.
