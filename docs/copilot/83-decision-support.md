# 83. Decision Support Finalization

## Read-Only Intelligence
The AI now possesses Intent Resolution, Entity Resolution, Structural Validation, Context Enrichment, Priority Scoring, and a generic Decision Policy Registry. It evaluates rules, assesses risk, and provides alternative paths—but it strictly **never mutates data**. 

It is the ultimate operational assistant, entirely configuration-driven and deterministic.
