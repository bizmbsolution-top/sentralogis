# 49. Operational Mood

While `Emotion` dictates the immediate reaction to a specific task, `OperationalMood` defines the macro-level posture of SentraBot.

## Moods
- `CALM`: Normal operations. SentraBot breathes slowly.
- `ALERT`: A minor anomaly was detected. SentraBot might pulse occasionally even when idle.
- `CRITICAL`: A major operational failure (e.g., 5 trucks delayed). SentraBot is visibly `PULSING` in the corner of the screen, demanding attention even if not actively in a conversation.
