# 60. Operational Situations

The `SituationCatalog.ts` defines standard anomalies and operational states.

## Situational Awareness
Instead of relying on the LLM to guess what "Driver has been at destination for 4 hours" means, we define standard situations:
- `DRIVER_WAITING`
- `WAITING_UNLOADING`
- `LATE_DEPARTURE`
- `MISSING_POD`

Each situation strictly defines its causes, its related terminology, and standard operating procedures (recommended actions). This ensures all dispatcher advice generated by Copilot is deterministic and compliant with company policy, rather than creatively invented by an AI.
