# 96. AI Core Completion

## Declaration of Completion
As of Phase 4A.7.4.2, the Sentralogis Copilot AI Core Architecture is officially **v1.0 Complete**.

## Frozen Components
The following architectural layers are now frozen and fully configuration-driven:
1. Intent Engine
2. Entity Intelligence
3. Validation Engine
4. Decision Policy & WhyNot
5. Operational Insight & Priority
6. Planner & Explainability

No further core abstractions will be introduced. Future development will focus strictly on operational integrations, enabling the Sentralogis Copilot to act as a production-ready intelligence layer for WhatsApp, OCR, and Live Trucking operations.
